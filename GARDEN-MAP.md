# Garden Map

This is the living index of **The Derivative Witness**. It exists to make the repository readable as one connected body of inquiry rather than a set of unrelated investigations.

The map should grow as the garden grows. Add each new investigation or substantive record here, then connect it to relevant existing records. Follow links to discover relationships; follow the relationships back to see how understanding develops.

## Emerging connections

### PHILO-001 — Philo and the later historical inquiry into choice

**Connection type:** Historical context; comparison pending.

PHILO-001 examines Philo's own first-century Jewish writings on divine sovereignty, received human capacity, voluntary action, and responsibility. FW-001 examines later writers, including Justin Martyr, in its broader historical trace. Philo therefore provides relevant earlier historical context for that inquiry. This link does not establish direct influence, agreement, or equivalence, and no comparison with Justin is made in PHILO-001.



### FW-001 — Historical theology and the Genesis foundation

**Connection type:** Open comparison; not an established equivalence.

FW-001 raises questions about the difference between human capacity to choose, willingness to obey, moral responsibility, and authority to determine good and evil. These distinctions may be relevant to a future comparison with the literal KJV Genesis 1–2 foundation in [God's Unified Architecture of Witness](https://github.com/JimmydaSciencePreacher/Gods-Unified-Architecture-of-Witness). The historical material does not itself establish how those questions should be resolved within GUA-W.

No direct connection to another Derivative Witness investigation has yet been established. Do not infer one merely from shared vocabulary.

## How to keep the map alive

When adding or updating an investigation:
1. Add its entry under **Investigations**.
2. Link the investigation to this map and to every relevant existing record.
3. Add reciprocal links where they help readers trace the relationship both ways.
4. Update **Emerging connections** when a relationship spans multiple records or connects a record to the wider inquiry.
5. Preserve disagreements, exceptions, and unresolved questions; connection does not mean equivalence or agreement.

The map is an aid to seeing the whole. It must remain faithful to what the records actually show.
