# The Derivative Witness

An open garden for investigation, discovery, and witness.

This repository is not a replacement for, revision of, or second version of any existing architecture. It is a place where questions can be followed, evidence can be examined, connections can be noticed, and understanding can grow.

## How this garden grows

- Begin with a genuine question, observation, source, or discovery.
- Follow the evidence where it leads.
- Keep source material distinguishable from observation, inference, interpretation, and hypothesis.
- Record uncertainty and unresolved questions instead of forcing conclusions.
- Let connections emerge; do not impose a structure before the material warrants one.
- Correct the record when better evidence appears, preserving what changed and why.

No conclusion is required in advance. No subject is required to fit a predetermined pattern. The work is to investigate carefully and leave a clear record that others can examine, challenge, extend, or learn from.

## First principle

**Attend to what is present before deciding what it means.**

The garden is open. Its growth should be visible in the questions asked, the evidence gathered, and the understanding that follows.


## The garden as a whole

The investigations in this repository are connected parts of one growing body of inquiry. The [Garden Map](GARDEN-MAP.md) links the records and tracks relationships as they emerge. Individual investigations retain their own evidence and questions, while contributing to and being read in relation to the whole.

## Continuing practice

AI contributions follow the [Standing Practice for Continuity and Witness Reflection](STANDING-PRACTICE.md): a small handoff at the end of every reply and four reflection questions at the end of every completed investigation.
